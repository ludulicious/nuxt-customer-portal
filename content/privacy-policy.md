Effective date: January 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the PortalNuxt open source project ("Project", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our software, website, documentation, or any hosted demo we operate.

## Information We Collect

- **Information you provide**: If you contact us through repository issues, discussions, or email, we may receive your name, email address, and any information you include in your message.
- **Usage data**: If you use a hosted demo, we may collect basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, and usage events to operate and improve the demo.
- **Cookies**: Hosted demos may use cookies or similar technologies for session management and preferences.

## How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Operate and maintain the Project and hosted demos
- Respond to questions and support requests
- Improve performance, security, and user experience
- Comply with legal obligations

## Open Source Use

The Project is open source and can be self-hosted. If you deploy your own instance, you control any data collection and are responsible for your own privacy practices.

## Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us host or operate a demo, or if required by law.

## Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

## Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Third-Party Services

Hosted demos may rely on third-party services (for example, hosting or analytics providers). Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

## Children's Privacy

The Project is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue or discussion in the project repository.
